- (decision) Visual direction anchored to the Dribbble import: soft pastel palette on a pale lavender ground, white cards, generous radii, diffuse low-contrast shadows, airy density, geometric sans, thin rounded line icons, light mode only.

- (decision) Surface treatment taken as-is from the import: pale lavender page ground #E7EAF6, white card container, each Todo its own rounded sub-surface with a soft diffuse shadow. Layered and tactile.
- (decision) Structure explicitly NOT borrowed from the import: greeting header, stat tiles (Today/Scheduled/All/Overdue), categories with note counts, sub-tasks, notification bell, timestamps, FAB, second screen. All are out of PRD scope. Only the surface language transfers.
- (decision) Completed Todo carries three redundant cues, never colour alone: mint fill + filled checkmark + strikethrough with muted text. Satisfies WCAG 1.4.1 (use of colour) and FR-2's 'distinguishable without reading the text'.
- (decision) Light mode only. No dark mode. Matches the import and the PRD's 'finish beats coverage'.
- (decision) Typeface: Poppins, matching the import's geometric sans. NOTE: Poppins' wide round forms need watching against long Todo text on narrow phones - check at the character ceiling.
- (decision) Error presentation: one fixed banner region at the top of the card, directly under the input. Owns every error - load failure, failed toggle, failed delete. Carries a retry affordance. Cost accepted: visually distant from the row that failed.
- (decision) Delete keeps the confirmation dialog exactly as FR-5 and UJ-3 specify. No PRD change. Accepted cost: with no text editing, fixing a typo remains a three-interaction operation - the PRD's own 'sharpest usability trade'. Undo toast rejected for v1.
- (decision) Input ceiling stays 500 characters per FR-1. Addition: a remaining-character count fades in at ~450 and is absent below that, so the hard stop at 500 is never mysterious. Additive to FR-1, not in conflict - no PRD change needed.
- (decision) Filter Views ship in v1 as FR-4 specifies, all three, visible from first run. PRD section 4.4's 'they earn nothing at three-to-five Todos' noted and overruled; UJ-1 depends on Active.
- (decision) Departure transition (FR-3): completed state holds visibly for ~400ms, then the row fades and collapses its height over ~180ms while rows below slide up. Total ~580ms. Cause and effect both visible. Must honour prefers-reduced-motion by cutting straight to the end state.
- (decision) Loading state: skeleton rows in the exact shape of real Todos, gently pulsing, while card/input/filters are already real and interactive. Zero layout shift when content lands. Satisfies FR-2's no-blank-screen and no-flash-of-empty-state.
- (decision) Voice: plain and unadorned. Short declaratives, no personality, no encouragement, no exclamation marks. Placeholder 'what needs doing?'. Empty states: 'Nothing here yet. Type above to add your first Todo.' / 'Nothing active.' / 'Nothing completed yet.'. Error: "Couldn't save that change." + Retry. Dialog: 'Delete this Todo?' with Cancel/Delete.
