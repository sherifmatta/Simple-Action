96px travel The second row has travelled the full reveal distance: it has moved left under the lane edge, so its checkbox and the start of its label are out of view. That is the gesture, not a clipped label.

The row slides left over a stationary action lane — the Delete panel is uncovered, not pushed in. Icon and the word Delete together, so the clay fill never carries the meaning alone. Releasing here opens the confirmation dialog in Step C; the swipe itself never deletes.

Reveal width 96px and the latch distance are not fixed by the memlog — see open question 1 in the style comment.

Motion, not instruction. Nothing to read, nothing to dismiss, no tooltip and no tour — which is what keeps this inside the no-onboarding rule while still letting a first-time user find the gesture without backtracking.

The panel above is frozen at the furthest point of the peek: ~24px of travel, enough for the left edge of the clay Delete lane to show and no more.
