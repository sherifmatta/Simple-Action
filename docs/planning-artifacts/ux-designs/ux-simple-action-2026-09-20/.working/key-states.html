create monthly deck for the sales review and pull in the pipeline numbers, the churn chart, the three case studies from last quarter, the revised pricing table and the headcount plan, then send it to finance for a sanity check before the board meeting, and book a follow-up slot with the ops team to walk through anything they flag in the appendix or in the forecast tab, plus whatever the support team raises about renewals and the two open questions on pricing 38

Rendered at phone width (362px card) on purpose: this is the check the memlog called for — Poppins' wide round forms against long Todo text on a narrow column. At 14.5px the text stays legible, wraps, and never forces the page sideways.

The ceiling is 500 characters. The remaining count is absent below ~450 and fades in past it, so the hard stop at 500 is never mysterious. Whether the input grows to fit (as drawn here) or caps its height and scrolls internally is not decided in the memlog — OPEN.

Counter format is open: a bare remaining numeral introduces no new word into the interface, which suits the voice. "38 left" or "462/500" are the alternatives. The memlog fixes the behaviour, not the format.
